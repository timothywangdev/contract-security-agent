## Solidity / Foundry smart-contract bug classes (append to vuln-scan brief)

In addition to the general categories above, specifically check for:

- **Reentrancy**: external calls (`.call`, `.send`, `.transfer`, token transfers to
  arbitrary/untrusted addresses) that happen before state is updated, including
  cross-function and read-only reentrancy. Flag `unchecked` blocks that remove
  revert-on-underflow protection around a balance/credit decrement that follows
  an external call.
- **Access control**: missing or incorrect `onlyOwner`/role checks on privileged
  functions (pausing, upgrades, parameter changes, minting, withdrawals of
  protocol funds); `tx.origin` used for authorization; missing checks on
  initializer functions (can be called by anyone, or called twice).
- **Accounting / share-price manipulation**: ERC-4626-style vaults where a
  first depositor or a donation/direct-transfer to the contract can skew the
  share price; rounding direction that favors the attacker over repeated
  operations; reward-per-token accounting that double-counts or under-counts
  across deposit/claim/withdraw sequences; share dilution from unexpected
  token behavior (rebasing, fee-on-transfer, fewer tokens received than
  requested).
- **Oracle manipulation**: price/TWAP reads that can be manipulated within a
  single transaction (flash-loan-funded spot-price reads, thin-liquidity pools
  used as the sole price source, missing staleness/deviation checks on
  external price feeds).
- **Unchecked external calls and return values**: low-level `.call`/`.delegatecall`
  whose success is not checked; ERC-20 `transfer`/`transferFrom` return values
  ignored (some tokens return `false` instead of reverting).
- **Delegatecall and storage-layout risk**: `delegatecall` into
  untrusted/upgradable implementations where storage slots can collide or be
  corrupted by the callee.
- **Integer/rounding edge cases**: division before multiplication, truncation
  that is exploitable via repeated small operations, off-by-one in loop bounds
  over user-controlled arrays (gas griefing / DoS).
- **Front-running / MEV**: functions whose outcome depends on the order of
  transactions in a block without a commit-reveal or slippage/deadline
  protection.
- **Signature replay**: ECDSA signatures accepted without a nonce, chain ID,
  or domain separator, enabling cross-contract or repeat replay.

Do not flag a finding from static reading alone as confirmed — reaching a
checked, replay-verified finding (reproduced against a fresh snapshot, ideally
with an independent trusted check) is what `csa audit` / the forked
`vuln-pipeline` are for. This scan is for triage-worthy leads, not final
verdicts.
